## What is Aegisora?

Aegisora is the **Zero-Trust Runtime Security & Governance Layer for Autonomous AI Agents**. 

As enterprises grant AI agents direct access to critical databases and production infrastructure, traditional security tooling fails to govern non-deterministic software. Aegisora sits between your AI agents and your systems as a real-time enforcement layer.

### The Binary Trap

Conventional security forces a binary outcome: **allow** or **block**. Aegisora introduces a third state for ambiguous requests: **asynchronous human escalation**.
