# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, maintainers, and leaders of the Aegisora community pledge to make participation in the project a respectful, inclusive, and constructive experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experiences, skill levels, cultures, and perspectives.

We are committed to creating a community where people can:

* Learn
* Build
* Ask questions
* Give and receive feedback
* Collaborate on technical problems
* Conduct responsible security research

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or abusive behavior.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness
* Respecting different opinions and technical perspectives
* Giving constructive and actionable feedback
* Accepting disagreement without personal attacks
* Taking responsibility for mistakes
* Welcoming newcomers
* Sharing knowledge and context
* Keeping technical discussions focused on the problem rather than the person
* Respecting project and security procedures

Constructive technical disagreement is encouraged.

Strong technical debate is not harassment.

The goal is to improve the project while maintaining a respectful community.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or intimidation
* Personal attacks
* Discriminatory comments
* Threats of violence or harm
* Deliberate humiliation
* Sexual harassment
* Unwelcome sexual content or attention
* Trolling intended to disrupt productive discussion
* Repeatedly targeting individuals
* Publishing private information without permission
* Impersonation
* Malicious interference with project infrastructure
* Retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report
* Deliberate attempts to bypass community or security procedures

Security research and vulnerability disclosure must also follow [`Security Policy`](./docs/security/validation.md).

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

* GitHub Issues
* Pull Requests
* GitHub Discussions
* Code reviews
* Project documentation
* Community communication associated with Aegisora
* Official project events
* Other spaces where an individual is representing the Aegisora project

Community members may also be held accountable for behavior outside official project spaces when that behavior has a direct and significant impact on the safety or integrity of the Aegisora community.

---

## Security and Responsible Disclosure

Security vulnerabilities must not be used as a reason to publicly shame or target contributors.

Potential vulnerabilities should be reported privately according to [`Security Policy`](./docs/security/validation.md).

Good-faith security research, including responsible testing, is welcomed.

Do not publicly disclose exploitable vulnerability details before coordinated disclosure is appropriate.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately to the Aegisora project maintainers.

For security vulnerabilities, use the dedicated reporting process in [`Security Policy`](./docs/security/validation.md).

For community and conduct concerns, use the project's designated maintainer communication channel.

Please include enough information to allow the maintainers to understand:

* What happened
* When it happened
* Where it happened
* Who was involved, when relevant
* Any supporting evidence

Do not share unnecessary private or sensitive information.

> Reports should be made in good faith. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves be treated as unacceptable behavior.

---

## Confidentiality

Reports involving harassment, abuse, or other sensitive community matters should be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Information should be shared only with people who need it to investigate and resolve the issue.

Maintainers should avoid unnecessary public disclosure of personal information.

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for addressing reported violations.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

1. Private guidance or clarification
2. A request to stop the behavior
3. A formal warning
4. Temporary restriction from project spaces
5. Removal of comments or content
6. Temporary suspension of contribution privileges
7. Permanent removal from project participation
8. Other reasonable measures necessary to protect the community

The response should be proportionate to the severity, frequency, and impact of the behavior.

Security-sensitive incidents may require immediate action.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

* Apply this Code of Conduct consistently
* Avoid conflicts of interest during enforcement
* Protect reporters from retaliation
* Keep investigations as confidential as practical
* Focus on documented behavior and evidence
* Explain enforcement decisions when appropriate
* Escalate serious incidents when necessary

Maintainer authority should be exercised responsibly.

---

## Appeals

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request a review where appropriate.

Appeals should:

* Explain the basis for the appeal
* Provide relevant context or evidence
* Remain respectful
* Be submitted through the designated maintainer communication channel

An appeal does not automatically suspend an existing safety restriction.

---

## Community Expectations for Technical Discussions

Aegisora is an engineering project and technical disagreement is expected.

Healthy disagreement should focus on:

* Evidence
* Security
* Architecture
* Performance
* Maintainability
* User impact
* Project goals

Do not turn technical disagreement into personal conflict.

A contributor can be wrong without being attacked.

A maintainer can reject a contribution without disrespecting the contributor.

---

## Final Principle

Aegisora is being built in the open.

A healthy open-source project depends on people being able to disagree, experiment, learn, and contribute without fear of harassment or retaliation.

> **Be respectful. Challenge ideas, not people. Build together.**
